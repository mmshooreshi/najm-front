# Najm Printing & Packaging Complex (مجتمع چاپ و بسته‌بندی نجم)

> Najm Printing & Packaging Complex (مجتمع چاپ و بسته‌بندی نجم) is a premier industrial printing and packaging manufacturer based in Tehran, Iran, founded in 1999 (1378). The facility operates high-speed multi-color Heidelberg Speedmaster offset presses, chemical-free thermal CTP laser platesetters, Bobst automated die-cutters, and automatic rigid box (hardbox) lines, delivering certified packaging for pharmaceutical, cosmetic, food, and export industries.

- **Machinery Fleet**:
  - *Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 106*: 6-color offset press with aqueous/UV coating tower, 18,000 sheets/hour with Prinect Inpress Control 3 spectrophotometry.
  - *Heidelberg Suprasetter 106 CTP*: Direct-to-plate thermal laser imaging at 2540 DPI resolution with hybrid micro-halftones.
  - *Bobst Novacut 106 E*: High-precision automatic die-cutting and blanking system with 300-ton cutting pressure and ±0.05 mm registration.
  - *Heidelberg Diana Easy 115*: High-speed automatic folder-gluer for crash lock-bottom and multi-point boxes up to 300 m/min.
  - *Thermal Soft-Touch Laminator*: Velvet, matte, gloss, and metallized thermal film lamination.
  - *X-Rite QA Spectrophotometry Lab*: Densitometry, rub-resistance, and board burst testing for international compliance.

## Key Production Parameters

- **Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)**:
  - Folding Paperboard Cartons: 1,000 units
  - Luxury Rigid Hardboxes: 500 units
  - Paper Shopping Bags: 500 units
  - Commercial Catalogs & Books: 300 units
- **Production Lead Times**:
  - Folding Cartons: 5 – 7 business days from proof approval
  - Rigid Luxury Hardboxes: 10 – 14 business days
  - Standard Commercial Stationery: 4 – 6 business days
- **Artwork Submission Guidelines**:
  - Formats: Print-ready PDF/X-1a or vector Adobe Illustrator (.AI)
  - Color Mode: CMYK + designated Pantone spot color channels
  - Resolution: 300 DPI at 100% scale
  - Bleed: Minimum 3.0 mm to 5.0 mm perimeter bleed
  - Die Lines: Separate vector layer with spot color stroke set to overprint
